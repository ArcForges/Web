# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence, give constructive feedback, and respect privacy. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. For a conduct concern, contact a repository maintainer through the contact method on their GitHub profile; do not post sensitive details publicly. Security vulnerabilities should instead use the private channel in `SECURITY.md`.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, reviews and other project spaces.
